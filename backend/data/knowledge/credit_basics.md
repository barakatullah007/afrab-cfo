# Credit Basics

## Credit Scores

A credit score is a numeric representation of a person's creditworthiness,
based on their history of borrowing and repaying money. In India, CIBIL
scores range from 300 to 900, with higher scores indicating lower credit
risk to lenders. Factors that typically influence a credit score include
payment history (paying bills and EMIs on time), credit utilization (how
much of the available credit limit is being used), length of credit
history, the mix of credit types, and the number of recent credit
applications. A good score generally makes it easier to get loans approved
and can lead to better interest rates.

## Inflation

Inflation is the rate at which the general level of prices for goods and
services rises over time, which reduces the purchasing power of money. If
income does not grow at least as fast as inflation, real (inflation-adjusted)
purchasing power falls even if the nominal amount of money saved or earned
stays the same or grows slowly. This is one reason long-term savings are
often invested rather than held entirely in cash.
